# Source authorization and privacy

This is a private owner import/review tool. Account ownership does not establish
permission for automated collection. No applicable TikTok method approval was
provided. Live TikTok access is NOT TESTED; automatic collection is NOT VERIFIED.

Permitted inputs are owner-controlled exports, owner-authorized screenshots,
manual evidence and explicitly labeled synthetic fixtures. The operator must
verify rights and applicable privacy rules. The tool cannot certify legal rights.

No login bypass, CAPTCHA solver, stealth, proxy rotation, private endpoint,
cookie extraction, private content, video download or comments harvesting exists.
The separate evidence worker makes one bounded page visit and stops on challenges;
it is not a complete channel collector. Only a local fixture demonstrates browser
control. Third-party resource requests are blocked; this may prevent real rendering.

No private owner screenshots or ten-row reference records were provided. Test
fixtures are synthetic, including URLs/IDs: they do not prove real videos exist.
Human confirmed URL fields in fixtures mean fixture evidence only.

Do not share this pilot as a multi-user service. Public release requires user
identity, tenant isolation tests, durable storage/jobs, retention controls,
monitoring and rate/concurrency limits. Screenshots may contain private analytics;
keep evidence out of GitHub and logs. Uploads stay in session memory, not a database.
